1. Information We Collect

To provide you with Bluetooth generator connectivity, status monitoring, and parameter configuration services, we may collect the following necessary information:

The information we collect is used solely to provide and improve our core services:

We implement industry-standard physical, technical, and administrative security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.
